## Design Context

### Users
Enterprise PKI operators, platform security engineers, service owners, independent security reviewers, and auditors working through certificate rollover evidence under change-control pressure. They need to inspect fingerprints, trust paths, dependency reachability, time windows, and reviewer separation without touching production key systems.

### Brand Personality
Restrained, forensic, and high-trust. The interface should feel calm under scrutiny, precise enough for a review meeting, and explicit about uncertainty and safety boundaries.

### Aesthetic Direction
A light industrial security workbench with ink and cool mineral neutrals. Risk red and verification green are reserved for status semantics; amber marks time-window attention. Avoid dark neon security-dashboard cliches, gradients, oversized marketing typography, decorative cards, and playful illustration. Dense evidence should remain readable rather than visually dramatic.

### Design Principles
1. Put certificate identity, trust path, and time evidence before decorative metrics.
2. Use color only to communicate validity, reachability, and review state.
3. Preserve forensic traceability: every state-changing surface exposes ownership and request evidence.
4. Adapt dense tables into usable mobile flows without hiding critical actions.
5. Repeat the offline-only, no-private-key boundary at the points where users import or simulate.
